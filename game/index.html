SOLMAR HEAT

An open-world crime story in sun-soaked Solmar Bay.
Rico Vega is back — and the whole city owes him.

HOW TO PLAY

WASD / arrows — walk and drive   Mouse drag — look around

E — steal / enter a car, bike or boat   F — punch

J — start the next job   M — minimap zoom   Shift — sprint

Steal rides, run five story jobs, grab the 12 hidden packages and 10 cash stashes, hit the stunt ramps — and stay ahead of the SBPD. Wanted level 3 and up means roadblocks.

An original game. Solmar Bay, Rico Vega and the SBPD are invented for this game; no characters, places or art are taken from any other game.
